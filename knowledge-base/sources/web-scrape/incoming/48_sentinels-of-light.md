Tips & Tricks
  * Generally only play from spots where you are confident you can get Vayne 3. Ideally you already have Vayne 2 before clicking the augment and can just play for Vayne 3 on 8.
  * Since Level 8 is more expensive in Set 16 and has lower 3-cost odds than prior sets, there’s a good chance that it is correct to roll on 7 for Vayne but this is uncertain until more games are played.
  * Can drop Swain for Shyvana as Arcanist is not important on this board.

Economy Strategy
  1. Level to 4 if strong.
  2. Level to 5, unless on 3 loss streak.
  3. Level to 6, unless it places you below 30g.
  4. Stay level 6, unless you can stay above 50g on 7 or you are below 40 HP and can stay above 30g on 7.
  5. Level to 7, roll until board mostly upgraded. Never go below 10g even if missing tank or carry.
  6. Build up to 50g, slow roll to 50g until 5-1.
  7. If below ~25 HP, sometimes you must roll until you hit here. Not ideal.
  8. Roll until you hit 3-star carry and tank. Can go to 0g. Only greed gold if you are above ~60 HP.
