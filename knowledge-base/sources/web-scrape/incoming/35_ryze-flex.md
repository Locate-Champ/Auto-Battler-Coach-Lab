Tips & Tricks
  * While there are many board states for Ryze, the Ionia version shown in the “Main” variation is the most common and performing the best this patch.
  * You can easily field a Targon unit for Ryze on most of these boards on level 10 if there isn’t a Targon unit on level 9.
  * For any Kennen + Kobuko variations, play from a Yordle opener and econ augments with Teemo as AP item holder. See the Fast 9 Guide on the website for more information. Transition to this board once you get to 9.

Bilgewater: Blasts explode on impact, dealing 25% of the damage as physical damage to adjacent enemies.  
Demacia: Execute targets below 16% (![scaleArmor Icon](https://tftflow.com/wp-content/uploads/tooltip/icons/scaleArmor.svg)![scaleMR Icon](https://tftflow.com/wp-content/uploads/tooltip/icons/scaleMR.svg)) Health.  
Freljord: Chill targets for 4 seconds and deal 60/108/194 (![scaleHealth Icon](https://tftflow.com/wp-content/uploads/tooltip/icons/scaleHealth.svg)) bonus true damage.  
Ionia: Blasts split an additional time, dealing 50% damage.  
Ixtal: Gain a random Region's effect on combat start and every 10 seconds.  
Noxus: Blasts now pierce targets, dealing 60% reduced damage per enemy hit.  
Piltover: Every third cast is Overcharged, dealing 40% more damage.  
Shadow Isles: Increase blast damage by 5% (![scaleSouls Icon](https://tftflow.com/wp-content/uploads/tooltip/icons/scaleSouls.png)).  
Shurima: Knock up enemies for 0.5 seconds, with a chance to dredge up Gold or Treasures.  
Targon: Heal the lowest Health ally for 10% of the damage dealt.  
Void: Ignore 30% of enemies' Armor and Magic Resistance.  
Yordle: Shrink targets hit, lowering their damage by 12% percent for 4 seconds.  
Zaun: Poison targets, dealing 15/30/500 magic damage 3 (![scaleAS Icon](https://tftflow.com/wp-content/uploads/tooltip/icons/scaleAS.svg)) times over 4 seconds.

Economy Strategy
  1. Without econ augment, fast 9 is almost impossible without win-streak.
  2. Level to 4 if you have upgrades and a good item to place on one of those upgrades.
  3. Level to 5, unless on 3-loss streak.
  4. Level to 6 if on 4+ win streak (scout to see if necessary in high elo) or can stay above 40g.
  5. Level to 6, unless you would go below 30g. If you have to stay level 5 due to low gold (rare), then level to 6 on 3-5.
  6. Level to 7 if you can stay above 30g, otherwise stay level 6.
  7. Level to 7, or 8 if you can stay above 30g.
  8. Level to 8. Main difference from Fast 8 is how much you roll on 8. Ideally you are high HP and don't need to roll at all. Otherwise, roll for acceptable board and accumulate gold afterward.
  9. Level to 9 if you are rich enough to roll 40g+. Generally only possible with economy augment or encounter.
  10. Level to 9 if you can roll 40g+ or if low HP.
  11. Level to 9 and roll for important 5-costs. For the remainder of game, roll until you hit your 2 star itemized units. Can consider level 10 after hitting.
