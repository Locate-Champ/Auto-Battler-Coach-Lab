Tips & Tricks
  * Ideally play from Draven opener. Can play around Freljord or Bilgewater early game. Try to grab econ augments and make sure to slam Gargoyle at some point so you can unlock Skarner on Level 7
  * Lucian & Senna are not picky about items. Ideally throw utility onto them and any AD items you have. Kindred is more particular and wants Guinsoo + Kraken + Flexible 3rd item
  * Heroic Grab Bag is only a strong condition if you can use it to upgrade Draven early. Do not take if you do not have a Draven yet.
  * Can consider light roll for Draven 2 on 7 (between 3-5 to 4-1) if you have Draven pair. Do not ruin your economy for this! If you’re uncertain of whether to roll, it is typically safer not to

Economy Strategy
  1. Without econ augment, fast 9 is almost impossible without win-streak.
  2. Level to 4 if you have upgrades and a good item to place on one of those upgrades.
  3. Level to 5, unless on 3-loss streak.
  4. Level to 6 if on 4+ win streak (scout to see if necessary in high elo) or can stay above 40g.
  5. Level to 6, unless you would go below 30g. If you have to stay level 5 due to low gold (rare), then level to 6 on 3-5.
  6. Level to 7 if you can stay above 30g, otherwise stay level 6.
  7. Level to 7, or 8 if you can stay above 30g.
  8. Level to 8. Main difference from Fast 8 is how much you roll on 8. Ideally you are high HP and don't need to roll at all. Otherwise, roll for acceptable board and accumulate gold afterward.
  9. Level to 9 if you are rich enough to roll 40g+. Generally only possible with economy augment or encounter.
  10. Level to 9 if you can roll 40g+ or if low HP.
  11. Level to 9 and roll for important 5-costs. For the remainder of game, roll until you hit your 2 star itemized units. Can consider level 10 after hitting.
