Tips & Tricks
  * This is the standard Azir Disruptor final board if not playing around Mel or Annie. Can itemize Xerath with AP items if you unlock him. Also can cap around Sylas if you can unlock him over Neeko
  * If you unlock Nasus instead of Xerath, itemize him as your tank over Swain
  * Senna is the best applier of antiheal and shred, but can also play other flexible 5-costs, Taric, or Skarner in this slot. Likewise, if you are unable to unlock 3 Shurimans or before you unlock 3 of them, you can play any flex units such as Senna, Fiddle, Skarner, Taric, etc.

  1. Without econ augment, fast 9 is almost impossible without win-streak.
  2. Level to 4 if you have upgrades and a good item to place on one of those upgrades.
  3. Level to 5, unless on 3-loss streak.
