Tips & Tricks
  * Play this similar to standard Bilgewater. In other words, play for tempo. Use your serpents on stats when you can’t buy anything from the shop that will immediately upgrade your board or move you up a trait breakpoint.
  * Likewise, this line will also prioritize at least one 7 Bilgewater item. Captain’s Brew is a bit different in this line. It has less value in end game, but it’s still strong midgame on TF so you should buy it most games if offered it just to be stronger Stage 3/4.
  * Other than that, you will want to toggle down to 3 Bilgewater (play 7 Bilgewater during rounds, toggle to 3 during planning phase when buying from shop). This gives you the highest odds of Illaoi, which you can 3 star to make your board stronger AND help unlock Volibear.
  * Make sure to remember to unlock Kobuko on 4-7 neutrals. If you are playing a standard 7 Bilgewater board from Stage 4, you might struggle to unlock Kobuko. Illaoi 3 + TK 1 + Fizz 2 will do it, so it’s not hard to get in the line, but you might need to play one or two extra units to unlock him.
