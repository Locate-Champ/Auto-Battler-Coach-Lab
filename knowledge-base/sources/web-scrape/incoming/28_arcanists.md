Tips & Tricks
  * Arcanists are not a comp you can play every game. It requires a ton of gold to hit and sell the Demacian units as well as hit the 5-costs on the max cap board. Most commonly play from Yordle opener + econ augment(s).
  * Aura farming condition is specifically for Annie
  * Annie is much more powerful when she has multiple mana items. Try to prioritize building mana items in the early game when possible
  * Lucian slot is flexible. Can play Taric, Skarner, Fiddlesticks, etc.
  * The default board is the most common transition from Yordle Fast 9 strategy (see the Fast 9 Guide on the website). The Azir and 6 Arcanist versions are options for late game if you have enough gold or if you didn’t play from Yordle opener.
