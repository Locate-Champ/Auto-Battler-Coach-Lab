Tips & Tricks
  * Play from Bilgewater opener. Make sure to put two items on Twisted Fate as early as possible so you can unlock Graves. This is NOT a reroll line. Play for either Twisted Fate 3 or Graves 3 from Bilgewater shop. In Patch 16.2, TF looks better than Graves. Pick ONLY one to 3 star.
  * Use your Bilgewater shop to make you stronger each round. Don’t sit on too many serpents. Prioritize purchasing stat upgrades and units that make your board IMMEDIATELY stronger OR IMMEDIATELY move you from 3 to 5 Bilgewater. Buy TF / Graves / Duplicators if no shop slots make you stronger.
  * First carousel priority is finishing an uncompleted item for tempo or Nautilus / GP if it provides 5 Bilgewater. TF wants Brew + Antiheal or Void + Attack Speed. Graves wants QSS + GS or QSS + Crit build. Keep in mind you can typically only play for one of the two of these units.
  * If you are 3 Bilgewater and have a 3-cost in your Bilgewater shop, wait before buying it. In an ideal world, you don’t ever buy any unit from your Bilgewater shop unless it immediately makes an impact on your board such as moving from 3 to 5 Bilgewater, 2-starring a tank, or helping 3 star.
  * On 5 Bilgewater, try to buy Captain’s Brew. On 7 Bilgewater, try to buy at least one item (costs 100 Serpents). Outside of these two item purchases, always reroll your Bilgewater shops on 3 Bilgewater which has the highest probability of 2-costs and duplicators.
  * This means you want to play 7 Bilgewater during combat, but toggle down to 3 (put the units on bench) when buying from your Bilgewater shop. After you hit 3-star 2-cost, Captain’s Brew, and one 7 Bilgewater item, buy stat upgrades from each trait breakpoint (see the 2nd to last tip for more info).
  * Once you get Tahm Kench, try to always feed every turn. Before Level 9 and your core upgrades, feed him “cheap food” like 1-cost tanks and AP units. Once you 2 star him or you are level 9 with a fairly upgraded board, you can feed him higher cost units.
  * Play 7 Bilgewater as long as you can. Drop to 5 late game once you are low HP or if you have 2 star legendaries to play instead. Flex units to consider include Kindred, Braum, Ornn, Swain, Shyvana, Volibear, Taric, Fiddle, and Skarner.
  * Buy stats at all breakpoints. There are 3 stats: AD/AP, AS, and HP. Each stat has its own tier at 3, 5, and 7 Bilgewater respectively. Whenever you buy a stat at a specific tier, it becomes more expensive the next time you see it. Your goal is to buy each unique stat at each unique trait breakpoint.
  * Bilgewater is a TEMPO comp. The opener is typically up many upgrades on other boards in Stage 2 and 3. Your 2-1 augment should help you win rounds, because winning rounds farms more serpents which snowballs harder. Do not play Bard, as he makes your board weaker. You should not be weak Stage 3.

Economy Strategy
  1. Level to 4 if you have upgrades and a good item to place on one of those upgrades.
  2. Level to 5, unless on 3 loss streak.
  3. Level to 6 if on 4+ win streak (scout to see if necessary in high elo) or can stay above 40g.
  4. Level to 6, unless you would go below 30g. If you have to stay level 5 due to low gold (rare), then level to 6 on 3-5.
  5. Level to 7 if you can stay above 30g, otherwise stay level 6.
  6. Level to 7.
  7. Level to 8. Roll for desired comp until stable. "Stable" changes depending on rank, items, augments, composition, and patch. Melee carries and tanks get the biggest spike for 2-stars. Usually stable involves 2-star tank or carry and some other supporting 2-stars. Try not to roll below 10g.
  8. If you are weak (e.g., have no 2-star 4-costs), consider rolling again to 10g here. If you are stable, save gold to go 9.
  9. If you are low HP and haven't 2-starred your carry and tank, roll to 0g. If you have medium to high HP with an upgraded + itemized 4-cost unit and some other upgrades, save gold to go 9.
  10. If high HP, consider waiting until 5-5 to go level 9. If medium HP, consider going level 9 if you can roll 20g or wait until 5-5. If low HP and can afford to go 9, do so even if you have almost no gold to roll on 9. If you can't afford to go 9 this game, roll every turn for upgrades.
  11. Level to 9 if possible. From this point onward, look to level to 9. Acknowledge games where level 9 is not possible and roll every turn on 8 to preserve placements.
  12. For max cap games, you can either roll on 9 or level to 10. If you are itemizing a 5-cost carry and it is 1-starred, roll on 9. If you have all your main itemized units upgraded, level to 10.
