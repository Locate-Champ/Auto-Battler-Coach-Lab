Tips & Tricks
  * Ideally play from tempo AP opener with Ionia Ahri (Prosperous Path is ideal) or with a Teemo Yordle opener.
  * In an ideal world, this comp skips level 8 and plays for Level 9 as early as 4-5 with enough econ. However, in reality, sometimes you will have to roll on level 8 before you can go level 9. If this is the case, check the Level 8 variations WITH and WITHOUT Kennen in the dropdown menu.
  * Ideal augment distribution: Econ (2-1) | Econ (3-2) | Item or Combat (4-2).
  * Making 1 Thief’s Gloves is high prio as otherwise you won’t have any items for Sett because you will want to prioritize items on Ziggs and Ryze. Ideal Stage 4 items would be 3 AP backline items, 2 tank items, and TG. Not possible to force, but this is how you should think about itemization.
  * If you play from Ionia Ahri opener, it is typically not too difficult to unlock Kennen on 3-7 if you play around Shen + Ahri + Neeko + Defender units. If you can’t unlock Kennen on 3-7, then play around the no Kennen level 8 board and make sure to unlock him on 4-7 by holding units in Stage 4.
  * Since the Level 8 Boards do not play around Caitlyn, make sure to hold a Caitlyn on your bench. It is not worth 2 starring her as she might cost you econ if you do that.
  * One of the hardest parts about this comp is making sure that when you go level 8, at some point you will want to unlock Sett. The turn you go level 9, you MUST unlock Ryze + Ziggs. Ideally you held onto any units you needed to play 4 region traits + itemize a Yordle or Zaun unit that turn.
  * Only roll on level 9 AFTER you’ve unlocked Ziggs + Ryze. If you made a mistake and forgot to unlock one of them, usually you must spend all your gold rolling that turn despite missing an unlock because you are too low HP. Only wait for the other unlock if you are high HP.
