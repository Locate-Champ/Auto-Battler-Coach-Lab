Tips & Tricks
  * You’ll want to play as many Bilgewater units as possible early game to get to Tahm Kench, but eventually you should aim to transition down to 5 or fewer Bilgewater + High Quality 5 costs. There are MANY variations. Use the dropdown box above the board to see them all.
  * For Stage 2 and 3, prioritize hitting 5 Bilgewater as soon as possible. This means prioritizing Nautilus or Gangplank off carousel if the other is in your Bilgewater shop. Feel free to buy 2 star units from the Bilge shop. Captain’s Brew is extremely high priority on Twisted Fate.
  * During Stage 2 and Stage 3, you should buy stats if you have nothing else in shop that spikes your board (upgrades) or moves you to 5 Bilgewater immediately. Remember that you must buy stats BEFORE the round starts for them to apply for that combat.
  * For feeding Tahm Kench, you should only feed him low cost units until you hit your upgraded board. 2-star 1-costs are especially efficient to feed him, but typically not easy to get. Remember to prioritize your econ for unit upgrades over feeding (no matter how much you want to feed).
  * Typically the earliest you’ll get 7 Bilgewater is 3-6 because you’ll typically level to 7 on 3-5 and unlock Fizz on 3-6. You’ll need Miss Fortune to hit 7 Bilgewater. She’s worth buying from the shop if you don’t have her yet.
  * If you are ahead of tempo, greed 7 Bilgewater and go for two 7 Bilgewater items + Captain’s Brew. If you are on tempo or behind tempo, play for one 7 Bilgewater items + Captain’s Brew then start dropping down to 5 or 3 Bilgewater with higher quality units.
  * Buy stats at all breakpoints. There are 3 stats: AD/AP, AS, and HP. Each stat has its own tier at 3, 5, and 7 Bilgewater respectively. Whenever you buy a stat at a specific tier, it becomes more expensive the next time you see it. Your goal is to buy each unique stat at each unique trait breakpoint.
  * If you are struggling to hit MF 2 or Fizz 2, roll your shops on 7 Bilgewater as they have the highest probability of offering Fizz / Miss Fortune as units.
  * Typically drop out of 7 Bilgewater once you find strong flexible unit upgrades like Wukong 2, Shyvana, Swain, Fiddle, Ziggs, etc. Sometimes 7 Bilgewater is strongest board if you don’t have any upgraded units to replace the Bilge units with, but eventually you will want to drop out.
  * Ideal gameplan is to winstreak stage 2 and stage 3. Never sit on more than 2 components and ideally never more than 1 (slam items aggressively even if suboptimal). Play 7 Bilgewater Stage 4 (often lose some rounds) and then spike in Stage 5 on level 9. Not always possible, but this is the goal.

Economy Strategy
  1. Level to 4 if you have upgrades and a good item to place on one of those upgrades.
  2. Level to 5, unless on 3 loss streak.
  3. Level to 6 if on 4+ win streak (scout to see if necessary in high elo) or can stay above 40g.
  4. Level to 6, unless you would go below 30g. If you have to stay level 5 due to low gold (rare), then level to 6 on 3-5.
  5. Level to 7 if you can stay above 30g, otherwise stay level 6.
  6. Level to 7.
  7. Level to 8. Roll for desired comp until stable. "Stable" changes depending on rank, items, augments, composition, and patch. Melee carries and tanks get the biggest spike for 2-stars. Usually stable involves 2-star tank or carry and some other supporting 2-stars. Try not to roll below 10g.
  8. If you are weak (e.g., have no 2-star 4-costs), consider rolling again to 10g here. If you are stable, save gold to go 9.
  9. If you are low HP and haven't 2-starred your carry and tank, roll to 0g. If you have medium to high HP with an upgraded + itemized 4-cost unit and some other upgrades, save gold to go 9.
  10. If high HP, consider waiting until 5-5 to go level 9. If medium HP, consider going level 9 if you can roll 20g or wait until 5-5. If low HP and can afford to go 9, do so even if you have almost no gold to roll on 9. If you can't afford to go 9 this game, roll every turn for upgrades.
  11. Level to 9 if possible. From this point onward, look to level to 9. Acknowledge games where level 9 is not possible and roll every turn on 8 to preserve placements.
  12. For max cap games, you can either roll on 9 or level to 10. If you are itemizing a 5-cost carry and it is 1-starred, roll on 9. If you have all your main itemized units upgraded, level to 10.
