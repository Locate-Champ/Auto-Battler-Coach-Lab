Tips & Tricks
  * There are two versions of Yordles: Reroll and Fast 8. This is the Fast 8 version. The main benefit of this version is that it allows you to get the grab bags from 8 Yordle much sooner, allowing you to scale into the late game. Can still hit 3 stars due to 4 Yordle effect too.
  * Unlocking Veigar makes a massive difference in the comp’s strength, so taking augments that help you unlock him can go a long way
  * If you unlocked Veigar, you don’t need to play both Rabadon’s Deathcaps on him. It is generally better to split them between 2 units as long as both are fully itemized
  * Volibear unlock does not require a 3 star. Kobuko 2 can unlock Volibear with Warmogs and any HP boosting item (including just a Belt) if you are playing 8 Yordle + Bruiser on 9. Voli doesn’t get played until 9 anyway so unlocking him on 9 is acceptable.
  * If you have a Yordle emblem, you can unlock Volibear on level 8 by placing it on a Bruiser unit and having Warmogs (or a combination of Sunfire and other HP items).
  * With Yordles, you should level to 8 as soon as you can field 8 Yordles, even if it tanks your econ down to 0 gold. This is because 8 Yordle grab bags provide enough gold to supplement the lost interest and you also benefit from getting stronger. Rebuild your economy using the grab bags and push 9.
  * When you play No Scout, No Pivot Yordles, there are two difficult moments you must be aware of ahead of time. The first is unlocking Fizz and Kobuko on level 7. There are only 6 playable Yordles before Fizz and Kobuko and you need to wait one turn on 7 to unlock them.
  * This means you should either level to 7 mid round (AFTER combat starts) so that you are not forced to field a 7th unit OR you can field a Yordle that you have paired but this is risky because it means you must ideally find the upgrade before level 8 so you can play 8 Yordle.
  * The second difficult moment comes with unlocking Ziggs, which has the same problem where you must be level 9 for a turn before you can unlock him. You can use the same trick of leveling mid round for unlocking Ziggs (level to 9 AFTER combat starts so you aren’t forced to field a 9th unit yet).

Economy Strategy
  1. Without econ augment, fast 9 is almost impossible without win-streak.
  2. Level to 4 if you have upgrades and a good item to place on one of those upgrades.
  3. Level to 5, unless on 3-loss streak.
  4. Level to 6 if on 4+ win streak (scout to see if necessary in high elo) or can stay above 40g.
  5. Level to 6, unless you would go below 30g. If you have to stay level 5 due to low gold (rare), then level to 6 on 3-5.
  6. Level to 7 if you can stay above 30g, otherwise stay level 6.
  7. Level to 7, or 8 if you can stay above 30g.
  8. Level to 8. Main difference from Fast 8 is how much you roll on 8. Ideally you are high HP and don't need to roll at all. Otherwise, roll for acceptable board and accumulate gold afterward.
  9. Level to 9 if you are rich enough to roll 40g+. Generally only possible with economy augment or encounter.
  10. Level to 9 if you can roll 40g+ or if low HP.
