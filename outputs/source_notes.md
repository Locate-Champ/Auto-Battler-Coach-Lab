## Mobalytics TFT Positioning Guide highlights (mobalytics.gg)

- Core fundamentals: place tanks/defensive units in the front rows and ranged/carry units in the back rows; melee carries often fit in the second row.
- Frontline indicators: high base health, higher armor/MR thresholds (30+ for 1–3 cost, 50+ for 4–5 cost), defensive abilities, tank traits (e.g., Behemoth/Bruiser/Warden), shorter range (1–2).
- Backline indicators: lower health/armor/MR, offensive abilities, damage traits (e.g., Reaper/Sniper), longer range (3+).
- Generic positioning rules: place ranged carries in the backline and adjust corners vs middle based on threat; strongest tanks on the outside to absorb early damage and protect carries; keep strongest tank near strongest carries.
- Spread vs clump: early game clumping can help focus fire; late game clumping is risky due to AoE and CC, so spreading out is generally preferred.
- Counter-positioning: scout enemy boards and reposition to minimize opponent threats while maximizing your own, including awareness of hook mechanics that target the furthest unit.
